## Suggested Alternatives

Prioritize eliminating `connect-busboy` and `promise-retry`, then replace
`memoizee` with a bounded cache implementation. Treat those as separate,
behavior-preserving follow-ups rather than expanding the vulnerability-closure
change. Keep `@usecannon/artifact-codec` internal and covered by integrity tests.
If `jsonwebtoken` is replaced with `jose`, preserve the existing algorithm
allowlist, issuer/audience expectations, expiry handling, and negative tests.
